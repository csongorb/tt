September 2006 somewhere in Germany we´ve sent a bee to a flight over the region.

After she left her safe colony she observes different things during her flight.

The forests are occupied by the bark-beetle and so the humans

have to burn their forests down. The bee visites some woodcutters doing their

sad work.

The bee flies on and comes to a violet acre where she observes an oversized

ladder, on which a fire brigade is completing its mission.

Explosions, smoke and action about nothing, because the catastrophe is invisible.

Finally the bee flies home.
