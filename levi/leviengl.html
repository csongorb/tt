The levitation leverage

I invented an inventor, who reportedly invented a flying machine in 1860.
I built the machine and smuggled it into the aviation exhibition of the Technical Museum Vienna. Exhibited nearby Otto Lilienthals flying machines it pretends to be a historically important exhibit : the old-looking photographies attest the possibility of human flying.
It is possible: a man can fly only by cognitive energy and tacit gravitation!
This way of moving throug the airspace is about not to copy the birds' flight, but to continue the upright walking of humans to the air. The vitrine with the objects fits perfectly to the museums' style and is exhibited there since 2005.
